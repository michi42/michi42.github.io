Left button: use the left inventory object, right button or mouse wheel: rotate the inventory (the wheel works in both directions). +/- mouse sensitivity (100%) · F1 sound on/off · F2 interrupt game · F3 restart level · Insert boss key (Atari Help) · F9 original size / scaled · F8 integer scaling on/off (no moiré) · F10 language (Deutsch / English / Français)
On phones and tablets: tilt to steer, tap to use the left object, drag left or right on the inventory to rotate it, tap the esprit logo for the menu.
